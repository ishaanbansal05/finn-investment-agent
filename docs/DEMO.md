# Three-minute walkthrough

Before recording: use a phone-sized viewport, refresh once if you want current sources, and keep the verified cases available. The demo requires no model key to show its core product judgment.

**0:00 — The attention problem**

“FINN starts with a portfolio, not an empty chat box. This is an 18-company mock portfolio with real EOD data and exchange filings. The brief ranks events that could change my thesis and keeps routine paperwork available below.”

Change the digest window to 24 hours. Explain the quiet Sunday window, then return to the week. Open one filing, show its priority reason and original NSE attachment, and save it. Avoid describing the routing score as a financial-materiality prediction.

**0:40 — The investor controls unusual**

Open Signals and tune the price threshold to 1%. The list immediately changes. Each volume alert uses the prior 20 completed sessions; the latest session is excluded. Explain that these are EOD triggers, with live refresh while the app is open.

**1:10 — The differentiating research workflow**

Select “Follow the evidence.”

“Infosys’s FY25 growth improved, but that’s not the same as meeting its final forecast. Management progressively raised guidance from 1–3% to 4.5–5.0%; delivered growth was 4.2%. Every row targets the same full fiscal year in constant currency. I can jump directly to the sentence in the call.”

Open January’s call evidence. Mention the acquisition caveat on early guidance. Do not call this a judgment of management honesty.

**1:50 — A second layer of analysis**

Open Cash quality.

“Cash conversion is 129.2% of profit, but excluding tax refunds management reports 112%. Both are strong; the adjusted view is less flattering and more useful. The cash bridge reconciles within IFRS.”

Open The numbers. Show that EBITDA is unavailable rather than substituting EBIT.

**2:20 — Generalizes beyond the cases**

Open live research and enter KPITTECH or another NSE symbol. Ask “Does reported profit convert into operating cash flow?” Show page-linked extracts, an answer if model synthesis succeeds, and any coverage limitations. The extraction can take a minute; keep a prior answer open if recording a strict three-minute demo.

“The verified case studies prove the product. This pipeline discovers documents for any valid NSE ticker where sources are available. It never fills a company dossier from model memory.”

**2:55 — Close**

“FINN is deliberately small: an attention queue and an evidence trail. My next step is to turn the promise ledger into a continuously updated record of management commitments.”
